ACCESS CONTROL FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about access control systems and installation in Kasaragod.

Do you provide access control systems in Kasaragod?

Yes. We provide access control solutions for offices, shops and commercial properties, including RFID, fingerprint and electronic door access systems.

What types of access control systems do you install?

Depending on the site requirements, we support RFID card or tag access, fingerprint biometric access and electronic door controller solutions.

Can fingerprint access control be combined with biometric attendance?

Suitable devices and systems can combine access and attendance functions. We can assess the workplace requirement and compatible equipment before installation.

Do you provide access control installation and configuration?

Yes. We provide installation, configuration, testing and troubleshooting support for compatible access control systems.

Can access control be used with CCTV?

Access control and CCTV can complement each other. CCTV can monitor entrances while access control manages authorized entry, subject to compatible equipment and system design.
