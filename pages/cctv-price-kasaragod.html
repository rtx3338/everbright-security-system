WHAT AFFECTS PRICE

CCTV system cost depends on more than the camera

A useful quotation should consider the complete system rather than only the camera price.

Number of cameras

A 2-camera home system and a multi-camera commercial installation have very different equipment, cabling and installation requirements.

Camera type

Indoor, outdoor, dome, bullet, IP, night-vision and higher-resolution cameras can have different prices and features.

DVR or NVR

Analog systems generally use DVRs, while IP camera systems use NVRs. Recorder capacity should match the number and type of cameras.

Storage

Hard-drive capacity affects how many days of footage can be retained. Recording resolution, frame rate and motion recording also matter.

Cabling & networking

Cable length, conduit, PoE switches, network configuration and the layout of the property can affect the installation.

Installation complexity

Height, access, drilling, existing wiring, cable routes and testing can change the labour involved in a project.

BUYING GUIDE

What to check before buying CCTV

1. Coverage: Decide which entrances, counters, parking areas and other important zones need visibility.

2. Night performance: Outdoor areas may need cameras designed for low-light or night conditions.

3. Recording: Choose recorder and storage capacity based on how many cameras you have and how long footage should be retained.

4. Remote viewing: If phone viewing is important, the recorder and network should be configured correctly.

5. Installation: Proper camera positioning, cabling and testing are as important as choosing the camera.

FAQ

Questions about CCTV price in Kasaragod

How much does CCTV installation cost in Kasaragod?

The total cost depends on cameras, recorder, storage, cabling, networking and installation complexity. Contact us for a quotation based on your property.

What is included in a CCTV system?

A typical system can include cameras, DVR or NVR, hard drive, power equipment, cables, connectors, networking equipment where required, installation and configuration.

Is IP CCTV more expensive than analog CCTV?

IP CCTV can have a higher initial equipment cost, but the right choice depends on the property, existing cabling, features and required coverage.

Can I view CCTV cameras on my phone?

Many modern CCTV systems support mobile viewing when the recorder, internet connection and mobile application are configured correctly.
